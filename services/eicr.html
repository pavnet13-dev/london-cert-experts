Frequently asked questions

What is an EICR?

It is a report on the condition of a property's fixed electrical installation based on the inspection carried out.

Does the price cover every property?

Prices depend on the property size and scope. Commercial installations with more than 12 circuits have an additional circuit charge as listed above.
