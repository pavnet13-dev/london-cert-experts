What is included?

  • Inspection and testing of the agreed electrical installation.
  • Assessment of observations found during the inspection.
  • An EICR report when the inspection and reporting process is complete.

Who may need an EICR?

Landlords, property managers, homeowners and commercial property operators may request an electrical condition assessment. Applicable legal duties and inspection intervals depend on the property and circumstances.
