Electrical Installation Condition Report

An EICR assesses the condition of a property's fixed electrical installation and records observations and recommendations. Suitable inspection scope and final price depend on property type, size and circuit details.

How to get started

  1. Choose this service and add it to your booking basket.
  2. Enter your property details and preferred appointment date.
  3. Our team will review the request and contact you to confirm availability and final pricing.

Prices shown are starting or standard guide prices and may be subject to property details, access, system size and any additional work. No appointment is confirmed until our team has accepted it.
