Information you provide

When you submit a booking request, the website may collect your name, email address, telephone number, property address, postcode, service selections and notes so the business can respond to your enquiry.

How information is used

Information should be used to respond to enquiries, assess service requirements, arrange appointments and maintain relevant business records. Do not upload sensitive documents through this starter website.

Storage and contact

If Supabase is configured, booking request data is stored in the project's database. For privacy questions, contact info@londoncertexperts.co.uk.
